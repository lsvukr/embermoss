NoteVision Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

In short: NoteVision uses the microphone only to find the note you play or sing, on your device, while it is listening. It does not record, save or send any audio, and it collects no personal data.

This policy describes how the Android app NoteVision (package com.lsvukr.notevision), published on Google Play by EmberMoss, handles your information.

Microphone

NoteVision asks for the microphone permission so it can hear the note you play or sing and show it on a musical staff. The microphone is used only after you tap Start, and only while the app is on screen; it stops when you tap Stop or leave the app.

The sound is analyzed in the device's memory, a fraction of a second at a time, and is discarded straight away. NoteVision does not record audio, does not save it to storage, and does not send it anywhere.

Data we collect

None. NoteVision has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not include third-party services that collect data. It does not send any information to EmberMoss or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Settings you choose in the app, if any, are stored only on your device. If you have turned on Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include these settings in that backup; this is controlled by you and handled by Google under Google's privacy policy. Uninstalling the app deletes them from the device.

Children

NoteVision is a general-audience music tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. A significant change, such as starting to collect any data, will be described here before it takes effect.
